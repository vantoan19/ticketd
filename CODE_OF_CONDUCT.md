# Code of conduct

We want people with different backgrounds and levels of experience to feel welcome
contributing to ticketd. This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other spaces managed by the project, including representation of the project
in public spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume questions are asked in good faith.
- Critique ideas and code with specific evidence; avoid personal attacks.
- Give constructive feedback and make room for newcomers to learn.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.
- Acknowledge mistakes and work to repair their effects.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, and publishing
someone's private information without permission are unacceptable. Repeated
disruption or retaliation against someone who raises a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting

Email [toan.tran.cqt@gmail.com](mailto:toan.tran.cqt@gmail.com) with the subject
`ticketd conduct report` to report a concern privately.

Reports should describe what happened, where it occurred, and any relevant links
or evidence. Share sensitive details privately rather than in a public issue.
Maintainers should share reports only with people needed to investigate or respond,
subject to obligations that prevent complete confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request a correction, issue a warning,
or restrict participation temporarily or permanently, depending on severity and
repetition. They should explain the decision and expected next steps to the people
involved when doing so is appropriate and safe.

A person involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome. Maintainers should
arrange an independent reviewer when needed. Someone affected by a decision may
ask for reconsideration with additional context through the private reporting route.
